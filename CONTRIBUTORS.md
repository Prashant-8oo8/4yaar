## 🤝 Contributor Guidelines

To keep this community welcoming and professional:

- **Be respectful:** Treat every contributor with kindness and professionalism.
- **Be authentic:** Share your own thought or a quote with appropriate attribution.
- **Give credit:** Never claim someone else's work, words, or identity as your own.
- **Respect privacy:** Only share profile photos and information you are comfortable making public.
- **Keep it appropriate:** No hateful, discriminatory, offensive, misleading, or harassing content.
- **Stay relevant:** Use a short message related to coding, collaboration, learning, creativity, or open source.
- **Keep the layout consistent:** Follow the provided card template.
- **Avoid spam:** Add only one profile card per contributor.
- **Respect licenses:** Only use images and other assets that you have permission to share.

Maintainers may request edits to cards that do not follow these guidelines.

## 💭 Share Your Thought

Your message does not need to be a famous quote. It can be a sentence about what motivates you, what you want to learn, or why you enjoy contributing.

Examples:

- "Great things happen when developers learn together."
- "Every bug is an opportunity to understand something better."
- "Open source is not just about code; it is about people."
- "Small contributions can create meaningful change."
- "Keep learning, keep building, and keep sharing."

Please write your own message when possible and credit the original author if you use an existing quotation.

## 🚀 Contribution Process

1. Fork the repository.
2. Create a branch named `docs/add-contributor-card`.
3. Add your card and upload your photo if needed.
4. Preview the Markdown on GitHub and check the links.
5. Commit your changes with a clear message:

   `docs: add contributor profile card`

6. Open a pull request describing your contribution.
7. Wait for review and address any requested changes respectfully.
